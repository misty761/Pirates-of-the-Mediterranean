‘Pirates of the Mediterranean’ is a witty visual novel that parodies classic pirate adventures while delivering its own original story of risk, greed, and freedom.

Step into the boots of Jack Staylow, a smooth-talking pirate who survives on luck, quick thinking, and questionable decisions. After a chaotic escape and an even more questionable voyage, he finds himself entangled in a dangerous treasure hunt alongside Elara, a sharp and experienced ruins expert. What begins as a simple chase for riches quickly spirals into a journey filled with ancient traps, rival pirates, and choices that shape their fate.

Explore mysterious ruins, uncover hidden secrets, and navigate tense encounters where every decision matters. Will you fight your way through danger, outsmart your enemies, or deceive everyone to get what you want? Your choices don’t just change dialogue—they determine the ending.

Will you chase riches, survive the chaos, or walk away with something more valuable?

Your story begins now.
